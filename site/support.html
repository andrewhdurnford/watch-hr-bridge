"Watch HR" doesn't show up in Zwift

  • Make sure you tapped ▶ in HR Bridge on the watch, and the screen shows a heart rate.
  • Keep the watch awake and close to the computer, within a few metres.
  • Turn Bluetooth off and on again on the computer, then reopen Zwift's pairing screen.
  • Allow Health and Bluetooth for HR Bridge when asked. If you denied them: on your iPhone open Health, tap your picture, choose Apps, then HR Bridge, and turn everything on.

Heart rate shows but Zwift says it can't connect

  • Only one app can usually hold the connection. Close other apps that are connected to "Watch HR".
  • Some computers struggle with several Bluetooth devices at once (trainer, heart rate, controller). A USB Bluetooth adapter on a USB 2 port often fixes it.

The green sensor lights stay on

The app runs a workout session so the watch keeps measuring. Open HR Bridge and tap ■ to end it.

Battery

Expect roughly the cost of any cycling workout on your watch. Start rides with a good charge.

Does it save anything to Health?

No. The workout is discarded when you stop. See the privacy policy.
